# fractional-information-gain

**Fractional Information Gain (FIG)** is a performance metric for knowledge-tracing
models in educational assessment, available in Python (NumPy and PyTorch) and R.

**Documentation (installation, usage, and API):
https://renaissancephilanthropy.github.io/fractional-information-gain/**

## Citation

Please cite this work as:

_Christie, S. T., Hauru, M., & Rafferty, A. N. (2026). Fractional Information Gain: A personalization metric for black-box predictive models. In Proceedings of the Artificial Intelligence in Measurement and Education Conference (AIME-Con) – Volume 1: Full Papers (pp. 109–116). National Council on Measurement in Education._
